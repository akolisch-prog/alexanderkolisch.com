Im Handel (Thalia, Omnichannel-Retail), bei Herstellern (SEVERIN, POSITEC/WORX), in Medienkonzernen (Bertelsmann, ProSiebenSat.1, EMI) und im Venture Capital (Holtzbrinck Ventures). Dazu kommen zwei eigene Gründungen, darunter steereo mit Verkauf an einen strategischen Käufer 2011.

What does Alexander Kolisch do?

Alexander Kolisch is founder and managing partner of advise by Webmatch. He helps companies make digital initiatives deliver results, with sparring and context at management level, and has more than 20 years of experience as Chief Digital Officer, Director and Head of Digital.

What is advise by Webmatch?

advise by Webmatch is the advisory practice of Webmatch GmbH and thus part of the Friends Digital Group. It supports management and digital leaders like a digital advisory board, with focus areas such as e-commerce, marketplaces, data, AI and organization.

Which industries has Alexander Kolisch worked in?

In retail (Thalia, omnichannel retail), with manufacturers (SEVERIN, POSITEC/WORX), at media groups (Bertelsmann, ProSiebenSat.1, EMI) and in venture capital (Holtzbrinck Ventures). He also founded two ventures, including steereo, sold to a strategic buyer in 2011.

What is Alexander Kolisch's educational background?

An MBA from ESADE Business School in Barcelona, ranked 7th in the Financial Times Global MBA Ranking and “Triple Crown” accredited, and a diploma in business engineering from Hochschule der Medien Stuttgart.
